Questions

Does Bid Studio write the application for me?

It drafts sections against each criterion and scores them, but your team reviews and owns the final text. Quality review is built in.

Which funders are supported?

Calls are modelled for Horizon Europe, UKRI, World Bank and foundation funders. Any call can be added to the feed with its criteria.

Is client data kept separate?

Yes. Each client workspace is isolated, with role-based members and access reviews.

Can I try it first?

The dashboard opens with demo data. Open it and try criteria extraction and scoring.
