Features

Everything in Bid Studio, grouped by what you are trying to do.

Primary

Grant & funding proposals

The core product. Calls from EU, UKRI, World Bank and foundation funders become scored, reviewable proposals.

  • Structured funding calls with value, currency, deadline and sector
  • Criteria and rubric extraction (/api/v1/grants/calls/{id}/extract-criteria)
  • Section drafting against criterion weights
  • Weighted score calculation and inline section editing
  • Submit to the quality review queue; export as Markdown
Core

Opportunity feed & libraries

One feed for open tenders, awarded notices and funding calls, plus the content you reuse.

  • Keyword search, notice-type filter and pagination with total counts
  • AI-style notice summaries
  • Answer library of verified responses
  • Evidence library of buyer-formatted case studies
  • Order tracking and email delivery log
Tenders

Tender writing support

When the opportunity is a procurement rather than a grant.

  • Clarification questions: flags vague terms such as TBD, "reasonable" and unstated weightings, and drafts questions
  • Pricing & cost narrative: reusable templates, margin and contingency matrices
  • Social value: Net Zero, Real Living Wage and measurable KPI statements
  • Win/loss debriefs: internal vs buyer scores and prioritised fixes
Data

Alerts & intelligence

Know what is coming and who wins it.

  • Alert subscriptions by sector, notice type, value band and keyword, with weekly digests
  • Buyer and competitor reports: spend, repeat winners and win-share concentration
  • Framework and DPS onboarding checklists with live completion
Teams

Workspaces & white-label

Built for consultancies serving several clients.

  • Client-isolated workspaces
  • Roles: owner, admin, writer, reviewer, client
  • Custom brand name, colours and domain per consultancy
  • Timestamped access reviews
