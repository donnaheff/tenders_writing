How it works

The same loop for every deliverable.

  1. Find the opportunity. Search tenders, awarded notices and funding calls in the live feed.
  2. Draft against the criteria. Proposals, packs and statements are built around each evaluation criterion and its weight.
  3. Reuse what you already know. Verified answers and evidence are matched automatically.
  4. Review before it ships. Every deliverable passes a human quality queue. Approval is never automatic.
